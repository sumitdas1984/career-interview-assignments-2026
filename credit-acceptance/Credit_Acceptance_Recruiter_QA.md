# 9. Answering Style

## Do

- Start with the answer.
- Keep recruiter answers concise.
- Use 1–2 concrete examples.
- Mention business impact where relevant.
- Connect your experience to the role.
- Sound positive about previous employers.
- Be honest about technology gaps.

## Don't

- Give 5-minute technical explanations.
- Recite your resume line-by-line.
- Over-explain Bosch restructuring.
- Claim expertise in every JD technology.
- Criticize Bosch or previous employers.
- Sound desperate about finding a job.
- Negotiate aggressively in the first few minutes.
