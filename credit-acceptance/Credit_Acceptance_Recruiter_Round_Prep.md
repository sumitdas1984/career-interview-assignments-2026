# 13. EoR — Questions to Clarify with the Recruiter

The JD says this is an **Employer of Record (EoR)** arrangement: you would be legally employed in India through an EoR partner, while working full-time and being fully aligned with Credit Acceptance. The JD also says the EoR partner provides locally compliant payroll, benefits and statutory coverage. The JD does **not** specify whether the employment is permanent/ongoing or fixed-term, so clarify this during the recruiter round.

## Highest-priority questions

### 1. Is this permanent or fixed-term employment?

> Is this a permanent, ongoing full-time employment position through the EoR, or is there a fixed contract term?

**This is the most important EoR question.**

### 2. Who is the EoR provider?

> Could you tell me which Employer of Record partner Credit Acceptance uses for employees in India?

### 3. What will my employment relationship look like?

> Will I receive a standard full-time employment contract from the EoR provider, with Credit Acceptance managing my day-to-day work and performance?

### 4. What benefits are included?

> Could you give me an overview of the benefits provided through the EoR, such as health insurance, PF, gratuity, leave and other employee benefits?

### 5. How is compensation structured?

> The JD mentions a CTC range. Could you explain how the CTC is structured between fixed compensation, variable compensation and benefits under the EoR arrangement?

### 6. What is the notice period?

> What notice period would apply under the EoR employment contract?

### 7. Is there a possibility of direct employment later?

> Is there any possibility that employees hired through the EoR could eventually transition to direct employment with Credit Acceptance if a local entity or suitable structure becomes available?

**Ask this only if the conversation allows time.**

## Additional useful clarification

- [ ] Who handles payroll, tax deductions and statutory compliance?
- [ ] Who handles performance reviews and promotions?
- [ ] Who would I report to — a Credit Acceptance manager or the EoR provider?
- [ ] Would the role have the same career progression and internal opportunities as other Credit Acceptance employees?
- [ ] Are there any EoR-specific restrictions on travel, working hours or leave?
- [ ] Is the position expected to continue long-term as part of the Credit Acceptance team?

## What the JD confirms

- Legal employment in India is through the EoR partner.
- The role is full-time and aligned with Credit Acceptance.
- Day-to-day responsibilities and performance expectations are consistent with global Credit Acceptance team members.
- Payroll, benefits and statutory coverage are handled through the EoR partner.
- The JD does **not** explicitly state whether the employment contract is permanent/indefinite or fixed-term.

**Do not assume that EoR means contractual employment. Clarify the actual employment term with the recruiter.**
